## Funnel Design

### Free → Pro Conversion Triggers
1. **Day 1:** Scan completes → if AI detects "Caution" or "Danger", show upgrade banner: "Full threat details are a Pro feature"
2. **Day 3:** Daily scan limit hit (20/day) → paywall
3. **Day 7:** "Your 7-day history expires in 24 hours" push notification
4. **Day 14:** Soft paywall on QR customization: "Add your logo — Pro only"

### Paywall Design (Proven Best Practices)
- Lead with Annual plan (best value framing)
- Show Monthly below with "most popular" badge (anchors against Annual)
- Lifetime in smaller text: "Or own it forever — $49.99"
- 7-day free trial CTA: "Try Pro Free" (removes purchase anxiety)
- Social proof: "Join 50,000 users scanning safely" (add real count after launch)

## Pricing Psychology

### Why $4.99/month?
- Below the $5 psychological barrier
- 42% savings vs monthly creates urgency for Annual upgrade
- Japanese ¥750 feels reasonable (equivalent to one coffee)

### Why $34.99/year?
- Roughly half the psychological annual commitment of $60+
- Monthly × 12 = $59.88 → Annual = $34.99 → 42% savings clearly communicated
- Most high-converting utility apps in this range ($30-40/year)

### Annual vs Monthly Mix Target
- 60% Annual, 30% Monthly, 10% Lifetime
- Annual plan is significantly more profitable (no monthly churn risk)
- Drive Annual with: "Best value" badge, savings % prominently shown
