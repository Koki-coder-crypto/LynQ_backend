## Category Strategy
- **Primary:** Utilities (highest QR app density — must be here)

## Rating & Review Strategy
1. Trigger in-app review prompt after 3rd successful safe scan ("We kept you safe 3 times — enjoying LynQ?")
2. Never ask after a danger detection (negative emotion kills conversion)
3. Respond to every 1-3 star review within 48 hours
4. Target: 4.7+ average rating

## Competitive Differentiation Keywords
These keywords are NOT in the names of major competitors — we can rank quickly:
- "ai qr" — no major app uses this in their name
- "safe scan" — unique safety angle
- "phishing" — security-aware niche with no dominant app

## Search Ads Strategy (Apple Search Ads)
Priority campaigns:
1. Brand: "lynq" — protect brand term, prevent competitor ads
2. Category: "qr scanner", "qr reader" — top of funnel, broad match
3. Intent: "safe qr", "qr phishing", "quishing" — high intent, low CPT
4. Competitor: bid on "qr tiger", "qr code reader" — poach at lower CPI

Target CPT: $0.80-1.20 (QR category avg ~$0.90)
Target conversion rate: 35%+ (utility apps typically 30-40%)
